SYSTEM SHEET · V1

Field Notebook — paper, ink, graphite

Everything is a sheet of paper on a desk. Structure comes from rules and stamps, not from shadows and radii. Corners are 0–3 px. One shadow value exists and it is almost invisible.

All four sit at the same lightness/chroma so no course shouts louder than another. Colour appears only as a 3 px spine, a 9 px square, or a bordered code chip — never as a fill behind text.
TYPE · THREE ROLES
Newsreader 500 · headings & task titles
15–34 PX · SLIGHTLY TIGHT · SENTENCE CASE, NEVER SHOUTY
Helvetica · controls, buttons, dense UI text
12–15 PX · NEUTRAL, GETS OUT OF THE WAY
Courier Prime · numbers, dates, stamps
9–92 PX · ALL LABELS ARE UPPERCASE + 0.18 EM TRACKING

MIGRATION NOTES · CURRENT BUILD → FIELD NOTEBOOK

Nothing loses a feature. Things lose their costume.

REMOVE
Gradient logo tile and the gradient page background — flat paper instead.
“Good evening, Danil” hero block. The date stamp plus one sentence about the real workload does the same job in a third of the height.
Layout switcher pills (Focus / Cockpit / Analyst / Custom). One good layout beats four adequate ones; keep the reorder ability in settings if it's already built.
Knowledge-garden illustration and the fossil/ammonite badges. If the reward loop matters, move it into Break room — it competes with exams for attention on the dashboard.
Big ring donut for “percent done”. A ledger line (34/95 · 61 left · 12 days · 5.1/day) is more actionable and smaller.
Glowing outlined circle around the timer digits, and the drop-shadowed rounded cards everywhere.
TONE DOWN
Radii: 12–20 px → 0–3 px. Only folder tabs are rounded, and only on top.
Card nesting: card→card→card becomes sheet → ruled section → row. Max one border deep.
Score chip: keep the 0–100 score, render it as a rotated rubber stamp so it reads as an annotation, not a KPI.
Saturated course colours → the four muted inks, used as spines and 9 px squares only.
Time-range pills (7d/14d/30d/60d/1y) → a single mono segmented rule under the chart.
KEEP
Unit-based task model, workload calculator, exam weights, session log, Obsidian daily-note writing — the whole reason the app is good.
Dark mode: same tokens inverted to graphite paper (#22201c / #2a2823) with warm ink (#eee6d6). Keep the four course hues, drop their lightness by 0.06.
Social tab is left out on purpose — send the screenshots and it gets the same treatment: a study circle as a ruled attendance sheet, names and hours, no podiums.
